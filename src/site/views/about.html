Our founder

Built by someone who lived the gap.

Lexora Africa was founded by Rudo Barbra Sibanda, an international business lawyer and governance expert with pan-African experience across corporate, regulatory, commercial and technology work. She practised corporate law, commercial law and litigation at DLA Piper affiliate firms in Botswana and Zimbabwe, and has served as General Counsel and Company Secretary for international multinational corporations in the United Kingdom and North America. For years she sat across the table from ambitious African businesses at their most important moments: structuring deals, raising capital, entering new markets and preparing for scrutiny.

The tools on offer were designed elsewhere, for different regulatory environments, ownership structures and growth paths. Rather than adapt them, she set out to build something new: not a consultancy with a login page, but a platform designed from first principles, supported by the advisory and training that make governance stick.

Our why

African businesses deserve infrastructure designed for how they actually operate.

The firm is headquartered in Kigali, Rwanda and operates across advisory, training, and technology. The advisory practice designs governance and compliance frameworks. The training arm builds the internal capacity for organisations to sustain those frameworks. And the technology platform operationalises everything into a system that codifies institutional knowledge, quantifies organisational health, and makes compliance a driver of growth.

The platform is structured around four pillars: people, projects, processes, and performance. Five modules cover the full operational surface of a governance-focused organisation. The proprietary GRC Health Score quantifies compliance standing, governance health, and risk management effectiveness in a single metric.

Advisory

Governance framework design, compliance gap assessments, corporate structuring, transaction support, and regulatory advisory.

Training

Good Governance Academy, AML/CFT compliance training, board and director development, and governance capacity building across Africa.

Technology

The Lexora platform: five modules, four pillars, one system of record. GRC Health Score, client portals, and full operational governance.
