The programme

Competence, not just documentation.

  • AML/CFT and financial crime

    Customer due diligence, enhanced due diligence, beneficial ownership identification, PEP and sanctions screening, transaction monitoring, and SAR/STR filing procedures.

  • Enterprise risk management

    Risk identification and assessment methodologies, risk appetite calibration, control design, heat mapping, and board-level risk reporting frameworks.

  • Operational and regulatory compliance

    Regulatory obligation mapping, compliance calendar management, policy implementation, internal controls testing, and regulatory examination preparation.

  • ESG and sustainability risk

    Environmental impact assessment, social governance obligations, community engagement frameworks, and alignment to international ESG reporting standards.

  • Data protection and privacy

    Data classification, consent management, breach notification procedures, cross-border data transfer rules, and privacy impact assessments.
