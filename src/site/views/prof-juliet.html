Juliet Kelechi

Systems Architect, Lexora Africa

Full-stack architect and platform engineer leading the design, development and delivery of the Lexora integrated business management system. She translates complex governance, compliance and operational workflows into an intuitive, scalable platform that serves regulated businesses across Africa.

Juliet leads the end-to-end technical execution of the Lexora platform, from system architecture and database design through front-end experience and deployment, ensuring that five integrated modules spanning AML/KYC, projects, finance, GRC and HR operate as a single, coherent system of record. She is also a committed mentor to women in technology.
