The programme

A curriculum for effective boards.

  • Director induction

    Onboarding programmes for newly appointed directors covering fiduciary duties, company law, governance codes, and board dynamics.

  • Governance masterclasses

    Deep-dive sessions on board effectiveness, committee governance, stakeholder management, and governance code compliance.

  • Compliance workshops

    Practical, jurisdiction-specific workshops on AML/CFT, tax compliance, data protection, and sector-specific regulatory requirements.

  • Strategic oversight training

    Strategy-governance interface, risk appetite setting, performance monitoring, and the board's role in organisational resilience.

  • Committee effectiveness

    Audit committee, risk committee, nomination committee, and remuneration committee operating standards and best practice.
