Rudo Barbra Sibanda

Founder, Lexora Africa · Managing Partner and Co-Founder, Upendo Tech Limited
“The shortest distance between any two points is innovation.”

An international business and projects lawyer, governance expert, entrepreneur and corporate trainer based in Kigali, Rwanda. With a career spanning more than a decade across multiple continents, she is recognised for strategic leadership and transformative impact across legal, corporate and training landscapes.

She holds a Bachelor of Laws (LLB) from the University of Botswana and a Master of Laws (LLM) in Extractive Industries Law from the University of Pretoria. She is a Fellow of the Chartered Governance Institute of Southern Africa (CGISA), holding a professional qualification in corporate governance practice.

Her legal and executive career spans diverse sectors and geographies. She practised corporate law, commercial law and litigation at DLA Piper affiliate firms in Botswana and Zimbabwe, and has served as General Counsel and Company Secretary for international multinational corporations in the United Kingdom and North America. She has led strategic initiatives across Africa, North America, the UAE and Europe, across the extractive industries, energy, infrastructure development, trade, technology, financial services and manufacturing sectors.

She is the Founder of Lexora Africa, a purpose-built governance, risk and compliance platform that unifies people, projects, processes and performance, giving organisations a single source of truth for data-driven decisions, sustainable growth and legacies that last.

She is also Managing Partner and Co-Founder of Upendo Tech, a licensed Trust and Corporate Services Provider regulated by the National Bank of Rwanda, where her work integrates technology with governance through corporate advisory, training and regulatory compliance.
