The Good Governance Academy is a non-profit organisation founded by Professor Mervyn King SC that collaborates globally to share information on critical business issues as a public good. Aligned with the UN's Sustainable Development Goals on collaboration and quality education, the GGA brings together directors, accountants, and governance professionals through events, endorsed training, research, and an inclusive online community. Lexora Africa is a support member, contributing to the Academy's mission of advancing governance awareness and practice across the continent.

The programme

What the Academy brings together.

  • Colloquia and events

    The GGA hosts two colloquia annually alongside fireside chats, roundtables, and workshops with global thought leaders in governance, sustainability, and corporate reporting.

  • Endorsed training

    Quality-assured governance training programmes endorsed by the Academy, with showcase webinars enabling direct engagement with trainers and course developers.

  • The ESG Exchange

    A global collaboration of sustainability reporting standard setters, regulators, professional associations, and educators developing practical playbooks for embedding sustainability reporting.

  • Research and thought leadership

    Collaborative global research on governance and critical business issues, published resources, and a curated book shelf of recommended governance reading.
